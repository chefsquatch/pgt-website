How I work

Agents that don't fumble the build

The usual problem with AI coding agents isn't bad code. It's an agent reporting work it didn't do, and you finding out weeks later. I've spent a year building a working method against exactly that, and wrote it up in public:

Directing Agents You Can't Audit: a field-tested protocol for building software with AI agents when you can't read the diff.

  • Every piece of work is checked against what it was supposed to do, not against the agent's report.
  • Small, testable steps with a written record of what landed and what's still owed.
  • Fast: a working first version in days, not weeks.
  • Plain-English updates. You always know what's done and what isn't.
